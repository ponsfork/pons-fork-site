Charity

Fees that give back.

Point your token's fee wallet at the Charity address. Pons Fork claims the creator fees and sends them — all of them — to the cause you choose. St. Jude, GiveWell, EFF and more. Every step is a public transaction on Robinhood Chain.

♥ 0% service fee — every claimed fee goes to charity

How it works.

01

Point

Set your token's fee wallet to the Charity address — at launch or any time after:

0x416C762f134B48Ef303bB0b8cAF7D1DB86F719d2⧉
02

Accumulate

Pons Fork claims your token's fees automatically and holds them for you. Nothing is taken — the service fee on Charity is zero.

03

Choose & give

Connect the wallet you deployed with and set your split — one cause, or any mix of percentages (even 5% each). From then on your fees flow automatically. Change it any time.

The causes.

Verified crypto-donation addresses, straight from each organization's official page.

Your tokens.

Connect the wallet you launched your tokens with. We'll find your pons deploys, show the fees accrued for charity, and let you direct them.

Wallet not connected — press “Connect wallet” above.

How your choice is stored

Your allocation is written on-chain: a 0-ETH transaction from your deployer wallet to the Charity address with your percentage split in its data. No accounts, no backend — anyone can verify it. The engine only accepts allocations signed by the token's original deployer, and percentages must total exactly 100.
