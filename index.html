How it works

One action from you.

01

Point

Set your token's fee wallet to the Agentic Mode or Cashback address above. Once — at launch or any time after.

02

Auto-detect

Pons Fork spots the token on-chain and waits until the fees are worth claiming, so gas never eats them.

03

Act

It claims, keeps 10%, and runs your mode — buy back & burn, or pay holders in ETH. Every step a public transaction.

⚠ Verify, don't trust

A token's dev can redirect its fees at any moment — to Pons Fork or back to themselves. A token is only truly connected while its status shows Fees → Pons Fork, checked live on-chain. Every claim, burn and payout is a public transaction on Robinhood Chain.
